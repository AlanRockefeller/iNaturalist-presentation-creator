Presentations from iNaturalist

Make a PowerPoint from your iNaturalist observations. Choose the photos, arrange them, and download a 16:9 deck. Nothing is stored on this server, so save your project file if you want to come back to it.

Sources

On iNaturalist, filter the observations you want (taxon, place, project, dates, quality grade), then copy the link from the address bar and paste it below. You can add several sources. They are combined, and an observation found by more than one source appears once.

    Tips

    • Any filter the iNaturalist observations page supports works here, for example taxon_id, place_id, project_id, d1/d2 dates and quality_grade.
    • Each source can match at most {{ "{:,}".format(limits.max_observations) }} observations, the most iNaturalist allows in one search. Add filters to narrow a larger one.
    • Thumbnails load directly from iNaturalist. Full-resolution photos are downloaded only when you generate the PowerPoint.
    Drag ⠿ to reorder. Click a photo to inspect it. Drag a photo to change its slide order. Keys: ↑ ↓ observations, ← → photos, Shift+← → move a photo, Space include, Enter enlarge.
